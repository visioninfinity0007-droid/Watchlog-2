## 2. Evidence inventory
Summarize canonical physical cameras, snapshot/event counts, first/last evidence times and any coverage gaps.
Do not list hidden transport/profile duplicates as cameras.

## 3. Visual extraction
Camera-by-camera, chronological, using the tenant's reporting/methods/visual-snapshot-analysis.md.
Preserve source snapshot/event IDs and local timestamps for material observations.

## 4. Business/security findings
Separate:
- observed facts;
- observed-derived facts;
- estimated metrics;
- unsupported metrics;
- unknown because of missing coverage.

## 5. Time / area / floor breakdown
Use only dimensions supported by this tenant's site type and confirmed camera mappings.

## 6. Analytics & camera quality
Record recurring glare, overexposure, obstruction, occlusion, blind spots, weak angle, mapping uncertainty, recorder/camera health and analysis gaps.

## 7. Recommendations
For every recommendation include:
- issue;
- evidence/repetition;
- impacted metric or risk;
- suggested improvement;
- confidence / validation needed.

## 8. Security incidents / exceptions
Keep routine activity separate from incidents. Do not infer identity, intent, demographics or wrongdoing from appearance.

## 9. Coverage & truth statement
State whether evidence is sufficient for a complete-day conclusion. Missing evidence is not zero activity.

## 10. Traceability
Reference source snapshot/event/report IDs for material claims.
